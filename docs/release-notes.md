# FRANKENTOWER — private creature-art playtest

Enemy encounters now use dedicated rat and bone heads, torsos, arms and legs as regular anatomy parts. New Rat Graft and Osseous Graft procedures let players receive those parts too; their hands remain compatible with equipment. Enemy health is normalized around each archetype's anatomy.

The game now opens as soon as cloud records load instead of waiting for every creature sprite to finish downloading and decoding.

The arena now stays visible between fights. Check the battle log if you like, then continue to Dr. Sutcher on appointment floors and the shop. Finish shopping to return straight to combat, without separate floor screens or page scrolling.

Each arm now draws back over its own weapon's preparation timer, then snaps forward in a consistent 0.12-second strike. A dagger winds back quickly and a club slowly; the arm position shows how close the weapon is to attacking. Ordinary damage and hit effects land at the visible contact point. Trigger chains retain their immediate activations.

Watch your stitched creature fight in a dark pixel-art dungeon. Each graft connects to its own painted torso socket: arm and hip buttons stay attached during motion, heads seat in the collar, and weapons follow the hands. The torso grows to make room for additional arm rows, heads, and legs.

Portraits, the dressing room and the arena camera now fit the whole creature, including wide builds and swinging arms. Creature artwork renders at higher resolution while the dungeon keeps its pixel style.

Creatures pursue targets, circle each other, close to melee range, and recoil from attacks. Legs determine physical movement speed; heavy weapons wind up before striking.

The arena takes centre stage during combat. Damage, critical hits, Poison, Bleed, Shield breaks, projectiles, and deaths appear in the room. Equipment and full statistics are available through inspection.

Between floors, equip anatomical sockets around your creature, buy loose equipment from the supply shelves, and visit Dr. Sutcher for reconstruction. The campaign, 51 items, 16 procedures, personal Legacy guardians, and cloud saves remain available.

This is an art-direction prototype: the room and equipment icons still use procedural pixel art, while the shared creature parts are an initial illustrated set. Room props are decorative. Further human playtesting will guide movement, economy, item balance, and the creature-art direction.

Created by Daniel Andersen for the Monster Mash jam. This version remains private/unlisted.
